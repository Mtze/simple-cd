# Users

This section is for developers who deploy their project with simple-cd.

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): onboard a repository, add `.cd.yaml` and the `cd-publish` action.
- [Deploying with the UI](deploying.md): artifacts, readiness, the deploy dialog, locks, previews, auto-deploy, rollbacks and logs.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): what the "not deployable" reasons mean and how to fix them.

Everyone who can log in sees every project, target and artifact.
Deploying needs a role, see the [permission matrix](../maintainers/index.md#permissions).
